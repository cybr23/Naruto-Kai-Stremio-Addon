# Naruto Kai Ultimate Subbed Edition — Stremio Addon

A Stremio addon for **Naruto Kai Ultimate Subbed Edition**. This fan project stream-lines the entire *Naruto* and *Naruto Shippuden* anime by removing filler, openings/endings, recaps, and padding. Each episode roughly corresponds to one manga volume (1–72).

---

## Quick Install

1. Copy the manifest URL:
   ```text
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cybr23/Naruto-Kai-Stremio-Addon/main/manifest.json

2. Paste it into the search/URL bar in **Stremio**.
3. Click **Install**.

*Or click here to install directly:*

[Install on Stremio](stremio://raw.githubusercontent.com/cybr23/Naruto-Kai-Stremio-Addon/main/manifest.json)

> **Note:** Streaming on Stremio Web requires a local Stremio Streaming Server running.

## Content Breakdown

### Season 1 — Manga Canon (Volumes 1–72)

* **Episodes 1–26:** Part I (Land of Waves through Valley of the End)
* **Episode 27A:** Day of Departure (Ch. 236–238)
* **Episode 27B:** Kakashi Chronicles / Kakashi Gaiden (Ch. 239–244)
* **Episodes 28–72:** Part II / Shippuden (Kazekage Rescue through Final Battle)

### Season 2 — Post-Series Extras, Movies & Novels

* **Itachi Shinden 1 & 2:** Light novel adaptation covering Itachi's childhood, ANBU years, and the Uchiha massacre.
* **The Last: Naruto the Movie (2014):** Canonical post-war movie set 2 years after Chapter 699.
* **Omake / Specials:** Kakashi’s Real Face short & Scroll of Wind exhibition special.
